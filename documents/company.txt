COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

Employees receive 24 paid leaves per year.

Employees can take a maximum of 2 consecutive sick leaves without manager approval.

For more than 2 consecutive sick leaves, a medical certificate may be required.

Employees should apply for planned leave at least 3 working days in advance.

Emergency leave should be communicated to the manager as soon as possible.

WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.

Work from home requires manager approval.

Employees working from home must remain available during normal working hours.

Employees must have a reliable internet connection while working from home.

IT SECURITY POLICY

Employees must not share company passwords with anyone.

Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

Employees must use company-approved laptops for accessing production systems.

Production database access requires authorization.

Employees must report suspected security incidents to the IT security team immediately.
